Quote currency, real onboarding data, and the questions we were missing

Three things from the onboarding audit: the calculator produced WRONG output for
Canadian carriers, most onboarding answers were write-only, and the questions
that actually change a quote were never asked.

CURRENCY (correctness bug). The engine hard-coded currency:'USD' on every return
path, so a Canadian carrier's quotes -- and their customers' emails and hosted
quote pages -- were labelled USD. Added Currency ('USD'|'CAD') +
currencyForCountry(), threaded from tenant.countryFocus through the quote, lead,
AI-preview and ingest paths, and persisted the real currency on the lead.
Display honours it everywhere: widget, hosted quote page (incl. accessorial rate
units), and email totals/deposits, all via Intl en-US -> $2,450.00 / CA$2,450.00.
The en-CA locale is deliberately NOT used: it renders CAD as a bare $,
indistinguishable from USD to the customer. Rationale is commented at each
formatter so it does not get "fixed" back.

This LABELS, it never CONVERTS -- the carrier priced their rate cards in their
own currency. Proven live: the same lane quoted by a CA tenant and a US tenant
returns byte-identical totals (8578.39), differing only in label.

The AI surfaces needed separate treatment because the model writes the symbol
into prose: both the reply/summary and the follow-up chat hard-coded a $ into
the model's OWN input, so it was copying it straight back out. Both now get an
explicit currency instruction plus a labelling safety net that only ever inserts
the characters CA and can never alter a digit. The chat net is applied BEFORE
the conversation is persisted, since history is replayed into later turns --
otherwise the bare $ re-enters through the model's own context.

ONBOARDING DATA IS NOW REAL. freightVerticals/pricingMode/serviceArea were
written and never read; the schema comment claiming they feed the AI context was
simply false. New buildCarrierContext() turns them into a prompt section (hauls
dry van FTL, reefer and flatbed; operates in CA, AZ, NV, ON; prices primarily
per mile) injected into the rate-card, reply and chat prompts. A tenant who
skipped onboarding yields an empty string, so existing prompts are unchanged.

THE MISSING QUESTIONS. Fuel-surcharge mode (auto EIA index vs a fixed %) and
who-sees-prices (public vs lead-gated) both change what every customer sees and
were silently defaulted. Now asked as step 3 of 5, plus OPTIONAL MC/DOT and a
public contact email as trust signals on the customer-facing calculator. All
persisted to their existing tenant columns; every field optional so Skip and
older clients are unaffected.

Verified: tsc 0, guards 0, 759 tests (was 702). Wizard driven end-to-end at
1280 and 375px -- 5 steps, multi-mode seeding 3 distinct rates, gating correct,
zero console errors, zero failed API calls, no orphaned chips.
