Questions, answered

Is QuoteFleet a TMS?
No. A TMS like Samsara or Motive manages fleets, dispatch, ELD compliance, and tracking. QuoteFleet does one job extremely well: turning your rates into an instant, branded quote page that captures leads. It runs alongside whatever you already use — nothing to rip out or integrate.
Why not just use a form builder like Jotform or Typeform?
Form builders collect answers; they don't price freight. QuoteFleet reads your rate sheet and calculates a real per-mile, LTL-by-class, or port drayage rate instantly, then captures the lead. A form still leaves you to work out and send the number by hand.
What about a shared Excel or Google Sheets rate template?
A spreadsheet can hold your rates, but your customer never sees a branded page, there's no lead capture, and every quote is a manual copy-paste. QuoteFleet gives customers a self-serve branded calculator and drops every request straight into your inbox.
How much does QuoteFleet cost?
QuoteFleet starts at $14.80/mo with unlimited quotes, and every plan opens with a 14-day all-inclusive free trial. No per-quote fees and no long-term contract. See the pricing page for the full breakdown.
Do you train AI on my rates or leads?
No. Your rates and customer leads are never used to fine-tune any AI model, and every tenant's data is isolated at the database query layer so your pricing is never exposed to another account.
