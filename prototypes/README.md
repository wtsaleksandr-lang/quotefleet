# Prototypes

Standalone, self-contained HTML design prototypes (open directly in a browser). Not wired into the app — reference for the real build.

## supply-chain-leads.html

Design prototype for **QuoteFleet Supply Chain Leads** — an ImportYeti-style importer/supplier lead-search surface, sold via QuoteFleet, tailored for truckers, brokers, freight forwarders, and product suppliers.

Modeled closely on ImportYeti's clean, minimal layout (their structure/columns/stats) with QuoteFleet branding (blue→teal accent, our name). Two views (Search results · Company profile) populated with **real ImportYeti Savannah house-BOL data** (headline stats real; some breakdowns representative for the prototype).

**Our differentiators over ImportYeti** (why it's worth paying more):
- **Winnability score** per lead (volume + switchable incumbent + recency + contact-on-file).
- **AI angle** per lead + an **AI Opportunity Brief** on the profile (why winnable, displacement angle, timing, who to reach) with a **suggested lane quote**.
- **Verified decision-maker contacts** (Hunter enrichment) + AI-drafted outreach from real BOL history.
- **One-click "Quote this lane"** to close lead → quote → deal.

Sections built to match ImportYeti's profile: stat strip, shipments-over-time chart, suppliers table, cream→green frequency heatmap, HS treemap, HTS table, top lanes/ports/containers/notify-parties/SCAC/states, FCL·bill-type·notify-party pie charts, recent-shipments table, and the contact-info tabs.
